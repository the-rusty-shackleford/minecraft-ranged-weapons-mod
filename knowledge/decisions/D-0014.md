# D-0014 — Steel is Metals and Materials'; our old id is an alias of its ingot

**Decision**: This mod no longer registers `rangedweaponsmod:steel_ingot` or
its recipe. It nests Metals and Materials (`[1.0,2.0)`), whose ingot fills
`#c:ingots/steel`, the tag every part recipe already took. `ModItems` adds a
registry alias from the old id to `metalsandmaterials:steel_ingot`, so a
stack saved under our id loads as theirs (NeoForge resolves aliases in every
registry lookup). The domain's `Blueprints` keeps steel's recipe as a *known
external* blueprint: `all()` no longer lists it and datagen no longer writes
it, but `maker`, `rawMaterials` and `ironEquivalent` still count steel as
three iron and a coal per three ingots, so the guns' cost ordering is the
same test it was.

**Why**: Vanilla Wheels needs steel by the ton and must not depend on a gun
mod; Rusty asked for one materials mod any mod can reference. The alias is
ours to register because the old id is our namespace. Coal leaves the list
of materials this mod's own recipes use; the tally still sees it through the
external recipe.

**Alternatives rejected**: *Keep our steel and let two coexist* -- two ingots
that do not stack. *Convert the tally to raw iron only* -- loses "steel is
three iron and a coal", which the ordering test relies on. *No alias* --
every steel ingot in the world vanishes on update.

**Links**: `domain/Blueprints` (`STEEL_INGOT`, `EXTERNAL`, `KNOWN`), `ModItems`
(the alias), `CraftingGameTests.steelIsMetalsAndMaterialsAndOurOldIdLoadsAsTheirs`,
Metals and Materials D-0001.
