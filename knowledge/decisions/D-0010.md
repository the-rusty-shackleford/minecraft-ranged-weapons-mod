# D-0010 — Rounds of one family may differ; the gun loads one kind at a time; damage matches the mod the players compare to

**Decision**: The shotgun's family gains a second round, the slug, which
changes the shot through the protocol's `rangedweapons:ammo` data map
(one projectile of 18, no spread to speak of, reach to thirty blocks).
The gun holds one kind at a time: `AmmoChoice` tops up with the kind
loaded while any remains and, when empty, takes the first accepted kind
in inventory order. Damage per projectile takes Another Gun Mod's numbers
(6 / 4×6 / 12 / 16 / 6), the shotgun's spread tightens to 0.07, and every
gun pushes (`knockback`: shotgun and slug 3, others 0.4 to 1.5). The
shots are synthesized without a single sine: a Friedlander blast pulse,
turbulent spray, near reflections, a band-limited thump and a darkening
outdoor tail.

**Why**: The players said the shotgun took three blasts to kill a sheep.
It was not the numbers but the game's hurt cooldown, fixed in the
protocol (its D-0009); with every pellet counting, the numbers could be
set to what the players compare against instead of compensating. Slugs
were asked for by name; "level 3 knockback" too. A magazine that mixed
kinds would need a list where there is a count, and the inventory-order
rule gives the player the choice without a menu. The old shots were
built on sine sweeps, and that is what an ear calls arcade; the new ones
are shaped by what a microphone records of a real shot, and were checked
by measurement (sub-millisecond attack, noise-like front, no dominant
tone, energy centred between 200 Hz and 1 kHz) since the author of this
note cannot listen.

**Alternatives rejected**: *Copying Another Gun Mod's sound files* --
its assets are all rights reserved; only its numbers are facts.
*Per-pellet knockback* -- six pellets would push six times. *A slug as a
separate gun* -- it is a round for the gun that exists.
