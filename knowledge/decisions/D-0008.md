# D-0008 — Guns are assembled from parts described once, in the pure layer, and written out by data generation

**Decision**: Every recipe is a `Blueprint` in the domain's `Blueprints`
catalogue -- ingredients as item ids or `#tags`, a pattern, a result, and
which ingredients in hand reveal it. `ModRecipes` hands the catalogue to
NeoForge's `RecipeProvider` under `./gradlew runData`, which writes the
recipe files and one recipe-book unlock each into `src/generated/resources`,
committed like the generated art. The tree: steel (three iron and a coal
make three), receivers of steel, a barrel of iron, a stock and a pump of
wood, a scope of iron and glass; then the guns in shapes that follow their
silhouettes, the pistol from two parts and the machine gun from four with
a two-step barrel. Steel is an item, tagged `c:ingots/steel`, by Rusty's
choice.

**Why**: Rusty asked for crafting that reads like building a gun, and
"intuitive" is a property of the whole tree -- the pistol cheapest, every
long gun with a stock, the scoped rifle exactly a rifle plus a scope --
which only a description in one place can be held to. Hand-written JSON
cannot be tested for those rules without a parser in the tests; a Python
generator would leave the rules and the tests in different languages. A
pure catalogue is tested with plain JUnit, written out by the loader's
own generator (which also writes the unlocks, needed because the pack had
no recipe viewer), and checked on a real server by a gametest that hands
every grid to the recipe manager and expects exactly our recipe back. The
game resolves a grid two recipes accept in hash order, silently, so the
part and ammunition recipes are also checked against every recipe in the
pack's jars by `devtools/recipes/collisions.py`.

**Alternatives rejected**: *Hand-written recipe JSON* — eight files already
drifting (no unlocks, `equipment` on ammunition), no rules. *Generating
from `devtools/art/build.py`* — the tree's rules would live in Python
while the tests live in Java. *Receivers from iron and coal directly* —
Rusty preferred a steel ingot. *Zero-cost parts to keep the pistol at
two iron* — a pistol that costs a stack's fraction of iron is still early
game, and the receivers are what make the tree read as a gun.
