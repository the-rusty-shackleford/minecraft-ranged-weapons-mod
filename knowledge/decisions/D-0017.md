# D-0017 — Bounded magazine text, readable parts and decoded audio headroom

Long renamed magazine titles visibly escaped the panel in the real booth. Title
and next-round text now preserve styles while ellipsizing to their available widths;
hovering reveals wrapped full text. The complete title remains the screen's title for
narration. Slot coordinates, Fill behavior, inventory locks and ordered loads are unchanged.
A real registered test-addon round with a long translated name is loaded through the
normal client Fill-button packet and server menu, then photographed. Test-only assets
are excluded from the shipped mod.

Original gun-part and magazine silhouettes receive a narrow upper-left bevel and
controlled shadow plane at sixteen pixels. Wood uses warmer highlights; magazine dye
masks and authored gun geometry are preserved. The new helper is deterministic and
strictly typed. Metals and Materials 1.0.1 is the preferred bundled steel art.

The old Vorbis files overshot their normalized PCM after lossy encoding: six clips
contained decoded samples above full scale, including a rifle peak of 1.442. The typed
exporter decodes each real result and reduces input gain when necessary until decoded
peak is at most .98. Passing first encodes retain their original bytes. This introduces
no new filtering, synthesis or compression and preserves the source recordings, timings,
and the deliberate pump/bolt body from D-0012. Every current clip passes, with no decoded
sample above full scale. This is a clipping check, not a claim that listening preference
can be inferred from the numbers. A before/after playback board is kept for review.

All work remains held locally; no release is authorized by these checks.
