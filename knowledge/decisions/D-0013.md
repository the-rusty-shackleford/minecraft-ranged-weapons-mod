# D-0013 — Magazines are ordered loads on the gun's stack; the gun's capacity is the magazine's; the protocol is untouched

**Decision**: Every gun but the shotgun is fed from a detachable magazine
item (`MagazineItem`: a family and a capacity), whose load is an ordered
list of runs of rounds (`domain.Magazine`, `MagazineContents` on the
stack) and which sits whole in the gun (`InsertedMagazine`, a component
wrapping the stack, name and dye included). The protocol's round store on
the gun is kept as a mirror of the magazine -- its count and its *next*
round -- after every change (`Magazines.sync`), so the fallback's stats,
falloff and firing, the HUD and the pillagers' guns all keep working
unchanged, and a mixed magazine changes what the gun does from one shot to
the next. Capacity follows the inserted magazine through a native-tier
weapon (`MagazineFedWeapon`) registered on the four magazine-fed guns,
which delegates everything else to the profile's weapon and keeps
`stats().capacity() == capacity()`; the profiles' own capacities become the
default magazines' sizes. R is a change (the first loaded magazine carried
goes in, the old one takes its slot), Shift+R a swap that walks the carried
magazines round-robin from the slot the last swap took
(`MagazineChoice`); on the shotgun's tube Shift+R unloads and loads the
next kind (`AmmoChoice.next`). Both are one pure rule in `Trigger`
(`START_SWAP`) and one flag on the reload component. The screen is a
container menu over the magazine in hand with five run slots and a Fill
button; nothing is ever dropped because the runs live on the magazine.
Loose rounds are adopted into a default magazine on first sight.

**Why**: Rusty, 2026-09-07: "all the guns except for shotguns can have a
new item class called Magazine that you have to fill with bullets. So its
on you to keep a mag with shells, a mag with slugs, a mixed mag, etc.
... label mags, color them differently ... The shotgun would be the
exception". The three choices put to Rusty and taken as given: the fill
screen with a Fill button; Shift+R for both swaps and a hotbar icon of what
is loaded; 15/30/75, two steel each, capacity kept a magazine property for
extended and double-stack magazines later. Mirroring the next round into
the protocol's store was the way to get mixed loads without a protocol
change: the store already answers "what round is loaded" to everything
that asks. The native tier exists for exactly the capacity override, and
it is the seam extended magazines will use.

**Alternatives rejected**: *A protocol change for magazines* -- nothing in
the protocol needs to know; a bigger contract for mobs that never carry
one. *Rounds as a flat list* -- a player loads handfuls, not rounds, and
five run slots say "mixed" more plainly than seventy-five. *Fill by holding
right-click* -- cannot express a mixed load. *Guessing the LAN-style
round-robin from slot contents* -- remembering the slot the last swap took
is one integer and walks every magazine. *Keeping the profile capacity* --
a 75-round box in a 50-round gun.

**Follow-ups**: 2.0.0 let the change time be the profile's
`reload_ticks_per_round × capacity`, which made the box's change 75 ticks
and would have scaled an extended magazine's with its size. Rusty: "Reload
time should not *directly* scale with capacity because removing and
inserting a magazine is the same action in any case ... Perhaps a
step-scale against capacity makes sense". 2.0.1: the change is the gun's
handling time (`magazine_change_ticks`, pistol 24, rifles 30, machine gun
50, the numbers the guns had before magazines) plus a quarter per doubling
of the standard capacity (`ReloadPlan.magazineChangeTicks`), taken from
the magazine that would go in when the change starts. The tube keeps the
profile's time. Extended magazines when asked; the shotgun's tube could
take a magazine screen of its own if kinds ever exceed two.
