# D-0019 — Six medium rounds in a built-in cylinder

Rusty requested a six-shot revolver with an animated cylinder and hammer,
stronger and slower than the pistol. They approved the concrete design:
10 damage against the pistol's 6, a 12-tick interval against 5, and R loading
loose medium rounds into a built-in cylinder. This adds one exception to
D-0013's detachable-magazine rule; its existing guns keep their feeds.

The revolver is a one-handed sidearm and fires once per pull. Its cylinder
holds six rounds of one medium-ammunition kind, using the existing internal
reload path (48 ticks), including partial top-ups and Shift+R ammo selection.
Creative requires and spends no ammunition or durability. Crafting uses an
upper receiver and barrel above a lower receiver, with no stock.

Ballistics remain profile-driven. A native capability delegates to the protocol
and records a cycle only after an accepted shot; player and mob callers share
this behavior. Chamber and firing tick are a persistent, synchronized stack
component, so each gun retains its own state through saving and handoffs.
Four ordinary baked models form the frame, cylinder, hammer and hinged crane.
Only the articulated parts move, with an eight-tick cycle that finishes before
the next permitted shot. No per-frame geometry construction or new network
message is needed. The model generator owns the original geometry; the existing
gun atlas and credited rifle shot recording are reused.

The protocol API and D-0018's separate materials dependency are unchanged.
This acknowledgement authorizes implementation, not publication or deployment.
