# D-0026: Everything a player carries feeds the guns, in either mode

Part of Rusty's one change for every mod that looks at a player's inventory ("all things that see
your inventory also see your backpack"): the Carried protocol (`minecraft-carried`, its D-0001),
nested in this jar. Asked whether D-0023's magazines mode, which kept bags out (the Fill button,
the tube guns and the magazines themselves), should reach them too, and whether rounds handed back
should follow the common give order: "yes".

Accepted:

- **Reach is Carried, in both modes.** `RoundSources` is now a view over `Carried`: the inventory
  (hotbar first), the offhand, then every carried bag's storage cells (mounts never). Kinds in the
  order first met, counts, and an all-or-nothing draw that takes the inventory's rounds before
  the bags' (a bag is the reserve). The feed mode still decides whether a magazine-fed gun wants a
  magazine or loose rounds; it no longer decides where they come from. `BackpackPockets`, the
  mod's own adapter over Backpacks+'s component, and the `Pockets` planner behind it are gone.
- **Magazines from bags.** A loaded magazine in a carried bag is carried: an empty trigger or the
  reload key takes it, after the inventory's. The swap order (Shift+R walks the carried magazines
  and wraps round, D-0013) numbers places in reading order: an inventory slot keeps its
  number, a bag's cell comes after them all at 1000 + 100 x the bag's place among the player's bags
  + the cell (`Magazines.Found`). `MagazineChoice` is unchanged: it was already rules over positions.
- **What comes back.** The magazine a change ejects goes into the slot the new one left when that
  was an inventory slot now empty, as before; otherwise where a give goes (the inventory's stacks,
  a bag's stacks, an empty inventory slot, a bag's empty cell, then the ground). Rounds a kind swap
  hands back, and a magazine handed back in loose mode, go where a give goes too, a bag included
  (D-0023 had said never into a bag).
- **The Fill button** counts, orders its kinds by and takes from everything the player carries.

Rejected: a flag to keep magazines mode inventory-only (Rusty chose one rule); putting an ejected
magazine back into the bag cell its replacement left (Carried gives in its own order; an empty
magazine landing where the player can refill it is no worse).

Verified: 60 GameTests with Backpacks+ 0.6.0 on the gametest server, green, the two loose-mode bag
tests unchanged. `everyBagIsAPocketInMagazinesModeToo` (the old `noBagIsAPocketInMagazinesMode`
reversed: the shotgun in magazines mode now counts 3 + 12 + 40 shells over the inventory and two
bags) and `anEmptyTriggerLoadsAMagazineCarriedInABag` (a box of 40 only in a pocketed bag goes in,
leaves the bag's cell, and the finger remembers position 1004). The HUD booth not rerun.
