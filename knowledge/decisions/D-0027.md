# D-0027: The counter reads loaded over total

Rusty, 2026-09-29: "The ammo indicator currently reads 30/30 for a full rifle. It should read X/Y,
where X is the number of bullets actively loaded and Y is the total number of bullets you have."
Asked the two things that wording leaves open, he chose "30/90: everything" (a full rifle with two
full magazines carried; not 30/60, the spare only) and "only what a reload reaches" (not every
round of the gun's calibre).

Accepted:

- **X is the loaded rounds**, as before, red at a fifth of the magazine or store. **Y is X plus
  every round a reload can reach** (`Stock.reserve`), so Y falls only as the gun fires, and a
  reload moves rounds from one side of the total to the other without changing it.
- **What a reload reaches is what the reload itself draws on** (D-0026): everything carried
  through Carried, the inventory, the offhand and every carried bag's storage cells. A
  magazine-fed gun in Magazines mode reaches the rounds in each carried magazine it takes (its
  handling's magazine tag), a stack of magazines counting each one in it. It never reaches loose
  rounds, which have to go into a magazine first. A gun loaded directly (the shotgun, the revolver,
  and every gun in Loose mode) reaches every loose round it accepts, of every kind, since `Shift+R`
  changes kind: four shells in the tube, ten carried and five slugs read `4 / 19`.
- **Creative reads one infinity sign.** It used to read `∞ / 30`, the capacity. Under the new
  reading, both numbers are unlimited.
- **The text is the domain's** (`Counter.text`); the HUD counts the reserve at most once a game tick
  (it draws every frame), again when the held stack or the feed changes. `MagazineContents.rounds`
  counts a magazine without building it, so the count allocates nothing per magazine.

Rejected: counting loose rounds of the calibre in Magazines mode (Rusty's choice: they cannot be
fired without the fill screen); counting only the loaded kind for a directly loaded gun (the
slugs are the player's too, a `Shift+R` away).

Verified: JUnit `CounterTest` (5). GameTests `theCounterReadsLoadedOverEveryRoundAReloadCanReach`
(a full rifle and two full magazines read `30 / 90`; loose rounds, an empty magazine, a machine-gun
box and a pistol magazine add nothing; the offhand, the worn bag and a bag in a slot count, a
half-spent magazine by what it holds; in Loose mode the same rifle reaches loose rounds, a bag's
too, and no magazine) and `aShotgunsCounterCountsEveryKindItTakes`. The HUD booth's photos with a
spare box in the bag and loose rounds beside it.
