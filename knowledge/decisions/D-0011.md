# D-0011 — The trigger is the attack key, cancelled outright; the use key is vanilla's

**Decision**: A gun fires on the attack key -- left click. With a gun in
the main hand the client cancels `InteractionKeyMappingTriggered` for the
attack key and its swing, on the press and on every tick vanilla re-posts
it while the key is held on a block, and sends this mod's press once; the
release is detected from the key's state, as before. Nothing is offered to
the target: there is no melee hit and no mining with a gun in hand. The use
key is not intercepted at all, and `GunItem.use` returns pass for either
hand, so right-clicking a door, a chest or a villager does what it does
with anything else in hand, and vanilla's item-use path (the hand-dropping
re-equip animation of D-0006) is never entered.

**Why**: Rusty asked for it on 2026-09-07: "I want the ranged weapons to be
shot with left click instead of right click so right click can still be
freed up for interacting with things. I realize this means you cannot mine
blocks with the gun equipped, and that's okay." Every shooter puts fire on
the left button, and D-0006's offer-the-click dance existed only because
fire and interact shared the right one. NeoForge's patched
`Minecraft.startAttack` posts the event before its switch on the hit result
and skips the entity attack, `startDestroyBlock` and the empty-left-click
hook when it is cancelled; `continueAttack` returns before
`continueDestroyBlock`. The booth's survival burst now presses the attack
key on a stone block, and the cracks in its frames are the bullets'.

**Alternatives rejected**: *Keep the use key and add a keybind for fire* --
two ways to do one thing, and neither what every other shooter does. *Let
the attack through on entities so a gun can also club* -- a melee hit on
whatever the muzzle happens to touch, on every shot at close range.
*Mining with a gun in hand* -- Rusty gave it up explicitly.
