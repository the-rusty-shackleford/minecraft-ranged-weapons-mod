# D-0003 — Recoil is a camera offset that recovers, never the player's rotation

**Decision**: Recoil is one immutable `Recoil` record on the client: a
current and previous (pitch, yaw) offset and a recovery fraction. A shot
payload adds a kick; each client tick copies current to previous and
multiplies by `(1 - recovery)`; each frame lerps the two by the partial tick
and applies the sample in `ViewportEvent.ComputeCameraAngles` through
`setPitch`/`setYaw`, and to the hand model's `PoseStack` in `RenderHandEvent`.
The player's own rotation is never written.

**Why**: The mod this replaces wrote the player's real look angles inside
the camera callback every frame, on a two-tick curve that each shot restarted
against shared progress counters; at a three-tick fire rate the aim
oscillated about seven times a second, which is the stutter that started this
project. An additive offset with exponential recovery cannot do that: rapid
kicks accumulate to a plateau and settle, there is nothing to restart, the
sample is continuous at any frame rate, and because the real rotation is
untouched, the mouse is never fought and nothing drifts when the offset
returns to zero. The kick magnitudes are the gun's handling data, sent with
the shot, so the server decides the feel and the client scales it.

**Alternatives rejected**: *Writing `player.setXRot` and undoing it* — every
frame's undo races the mouse. *A bezier over a fixed duration per shot* — the
restart problem. *Server-side view packets* — a round trip of latency per
kick and a fight with the client's own prediction.
