# D-0012 — Every sound is a cut of a CC0 field recording; synthesis is retired

**Decision**: The nine sounds are cut from recordings of real firearms --
michorvath's 9mm, AR-15 and 20-gauge from one range session, a Mossberg
500's pump, a 1903 Springfield in .30-06 with a Mauser 98k's bolt over it,
a .303 Lee-Enfield for the machine gun's short report, an M110 with its
echo for the distant report, a Ruger dry-fired, a 1911's magazine dropping
and the 9mm's magazine seated and chambered -- each dedicated to the public
domain under CC0 on freesound.org. `build.py` no longer synthesizes: it
cuts, re-times, fades, mixes and normalizes (`take`, `assemble`,
`RECORDINGS`). The recordings themselves are committed under
`devtools/art/sounds/src/` with `SOURCES.md` crediting each recordist.
Only CC0 material is used, so the repository's public-domain assets need no
terms and the code stays AGPL.

**Why**: Rusty's friends, after the third synthesis ("the sounds are
generally great" and then "still kinda fruity"): a model measured as
realistic is still heard as a game. Rusty offered Mixkit and Pixabay and
said to commit the files. Mixkit's licence forbids redistributing an item
"with source files" and Pixabay's forbids standalone distribution -- both
awkward for a public repository -- and Pixabay's gun clips turned out to be
mirrors of freesound's CC0 collection anyway, so the originals were taken
from there, where the dedication is explicit on every page (each was read
before download). Choosing blind: descriptions separated field recordings
from designed mixes (layered, pitched, "door smash" added), and
measurements separated single clean reports from bursts, plateaus (a
recorder's limiter) and noisy floors. The pump and the bolt sit a second
after their shots in the recordings and were moved to 0.29/0.53 s and
0.22/0.50 s, inside the guns' cycle times, at a quarter gain.

**Alternatives rejected**: *A fourth synthesis* -- the measurements had
already said realistic three times. *Mixkit's clips* -- one real gunshot
in the whole category, and the licence question. *Sounds fetched at build
time and kept out of the repo* -- the CC0 originals made it unnecessary.
*Freesound's API* -- needs a key; the high-quality previews are served to
anyone, and a recording's own page carries the licence.

**Follow-ups**: Rusty's friends, 2026-09-08: the machine gun "wimpy",
the scoped rifle "too quiet and distant", and "a rack sound in between
shots" wanted for the shotgun. 2.1.0: the machine gun is an M240 single
shot from U.S. Army footage (the heaviest clip found, two fifths of its
first 150 ms below 250 Hz), the scoped rifle the first shot of an H&K G3
in 7.62 recorded close outdoors; the .303 and the noise-reduced .30-06 are
gone. The pump and the bolt are no longer mixed under the report at a
quarter gain: each is its own sound (`shotgun_pump`, `bolt`) named in the
gun's handling as `cycle_sound` and played `cycle_delay_ticks` (5) after
every shot by `PlayerGunnery`, on its own tick. 2.2.0: the rack was "a
bit too quiet" (its strokes are clicks 28 dB above their own body, so at
a report's peak the body was nothing): both action sounds are driven into
soft clipping to bring the body up, and played at full volume; the scoped
rifle "shoots a bit too fast" -- its `fire_rate_ticks` is 20 so the bolt
finishes before the next shot, the shotgun's 15 for the pump. No real
recording heavier than the M240 was found. freesound's TLS
certificate had lapsed the evening the first files were fetched (expired 2026-09-07 19:28 UTC); they were fetched
without verification and their hashes should be compared against a
verified fetch once it renews.
