# D-0015 — The one-handed arm is the crossbow's trigger arm, number for number

**Decision**: `ArmPoses.PISTOL` poses the holding arm with exactly the
angles the game gives the crossbow's trigger arm in
`AnimationUtils.animateCrossbowHold`: yaw `-0.3F + head.yRot` (mirrored
for the left arm), pitch `-π/2 + head.xRot + 0.1F`, roll 0, computed in
the game's own order of float operations by `domain/HoldOut.triggerArm`.
The other arm is left as the game has it. `HoldOut.recognised` encodes the
fingerprint by which Fresh Animations: Player Extension (v1.1) takes an arm
for the crossbow's trigger arm -- yaw within 1e-4, pitch within 0.0501,
the pitch slack being the idle bob the game adds after posing.
`HoldOutTest` pins the identity to the bit and the recognition at every
head angle; the photo booth reads the trigger arm off the rendered model
at every gun's third-person frames and fails on a mismatch.

**Why**: Rusty (2026-09-16): the pistol is "in hand but static down by the
side as opposed to dynamically held out and forward" on their client.
Reproduced in the booth one variable at a time, the arm's angles logged
beside each frame. With nothing but the mod, the arm is out (pitch -1.377,
yaw -0.100: the old pose plus the bob). With the pack's Not Enough
Animations 1.12.4 in `mods/`, unchanged to the bit: NEA recomputes arm
poses from vanilla's item-use state, finds none on a gun, and leaves the
model alone, so it was never the cause. With Entity Model Features 3.2.4,
Entity Texture Features 7.1 and Fresh Animations: Player Extension 1.1
enabled, pitch -0.300, yaw -0.135: the pack's own "holding an item" pose
(`a_player_equipment.jpm`), which it gives any arm whose angles match none
of the fingerprints in `a_player_variables.jpm`. Those fingerprints are
numeric -- crossbow hold and charge, bow, spyglass, horn, trident, brush,
shield -- and most also require vanilla's item-use state, which D-0011
keeps off our guns. The crossbow hold's is the one recognisable without
use state, checked on the trigger arm alone, and it is the game's own
"one arm out along the look"; so the pistol's arm speaks it. Under the
pack the arm is then passed through from vanilla
(`var.RarmRX_drag * ractionT²`), and the other arm with it
(`varb.Laction` includes `rcrossbow_hold`), giving the one-armed aim.
Under vanilla or NEA the change is 0.2 rad of yaw toward the body's
centre and 0.1 rad less lift, judged in the booth's frames.

**Alternatives rejected**: *A Not Enough Animations `BasicAnimation`* (the
plan of 2026-09-16) -- NEA is transparent to the pose, so the compat would
fix nothing, and it cannot reach the pack, which reads numbers, not NEA's
API. *Faking vanilla use state* to match the spyglass or bow fingerprints
-- D-0011 keeps the guns out of the use path for interactions' sake and the
re-equip animation's. *Shipping an override of the pack's variables* --
its terms forbid redistribution, and the pack is the player's, not the
mod's. *Leaving the pistol as it was* -- an arm at the hip is the hang
D-0009 was made to end.
