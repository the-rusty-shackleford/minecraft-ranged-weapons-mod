# D-0028: The rocket launcher

Rusty, 2026-10-01: "I want you to design a rocket launcher with lock on capability, added to ranged
weapons. It should be kind of expensive to craft, and its rockets should also be kinda expensive. I
think holding right click while maintaining reticle contact with target should lock in on it, and
once locked on, left click fires a rocket that hones in. You can also launch a rocket straight if
you skip the lock on."

Then:
- "Lock on should only work against valid targets (any creature or mob or whatever that you could do damage to, not inanimate blocks). Otherwise, normal vanilla interactions when right clicking."
- "reticle contact should not be needed once you've completed lock on, only while actively locking on."
- "vehicles should also be targetable."

His three calls on the plan:
- The blast breaks blocks, behind a server switch, as Dynamite's does.
- A lock holds until it is fired or lost.
- Astra does the art and the sound to a brief.

## The decision

**The launcher is a gun.** It is a `LauncherItem` (a `GunItem`): two hands, one rocket loaded directly, fired on the attack key through the same trigger, reload, counter and creative rules as every gun. Its protocol class is `launcher`, which the protocol already had. Its native capability, `LauncherWeapon`, is the revolver's shape:
- ammunition and numbers come from the profile, through the protocol's `ProfiledWeapon`;
- `fire` launches a `Rocket` instead of bullets. The protocol's contract says only "spawns projectiles", so it needs no change.

A mob given the launcher by some other mod fires straight. Armed Pillagers' loadouts are explicit lists, so pillagers never get one.

**The seeker is the launcher's sight.** With the launcher in the main hand, the use key raises the sight, as the aim key does on every gun. Raising it sends the existing aim message, so there is no new payload; the plan had proposed one.
- **D-0011 stands.** The use key is not intercepted: a door, chest or villager in reach gets its interaction, and the sight only reads that the key is down.
- **One addition:** the off hand's item use is refused while a launcher is in the main hand. A shield or a meal there would put the player "in use", and vanilla discards attack clicks while an item is in use, so the trigger would die.

**Locking** is the pure `domain/Seeker`, stepped on the server each tick:
- **Candidate:** the valid target whose edge is within 2.5° of the look, within 128 blocks, that the eye can see. The search walks the look in eight 16-block segments, so the server never queries a 128-block cube.
- **Acquiring:** 30 ticks of contact lock it. A lapse of up to 4 ticks is forgiven. Another target starts over, and lowering the sight ends the acquisition.
- **Holding:** once made, a lock needs neither reticle nor sight. It ends when:
  - a rocket is launched;
  - the launcher is put away;
  - the target dies, leaves the level, goes beyond 128 blocks or stops being valid;
  - or a new acquisition completes on another target.
- **Sync:** the seeker lives in the attachment `ModData.LOCK`, sent only to its holder for the HUD and the tones.

**A valid target** is the pure `domain/TargetFacts`, fed by `Targets`. It is something alive and attackable, not invulnerable, and not the shooter or anything in their vehicle, that is either:
- a **creature**: any living entity but an armour stand; or
- a **vehicle**: anything built on vanilla's vehicle class, or an entity type in the tag `rangedweaponsmod:lockable_vehicles`.

The vanilla class covers boats, minecarts, Vanilla Wheels' vehicles and Shippy Ships' ships. The tag covers the vehicles in the pack that don't extend vanilla's class, all optional entries:
- Immersive Aircraft's seven aircraft;
- Man of Many Planes' two planes;
- Automobility's car.

A player counts only in survival or adventure, and only when the shooter may hurt them, as vanilla decides it (the server's PvP and team friendly fire).

**A rocket** flies on the server (`Rocket`, with the pure `domain/Motor` and `domain/Guidance`):
- **Motor:** it leaves the tube at 0.6 blocks a tick and doesn't steer for 3 ticks. The motor then lights and it accelerates to 2.5 blocks a tick by tick 15. It has no gravity, and detonates at tick 160.
- **Guidance:** a guided rocket aims at the point where it would meet its target if the target kept its velocity, and turns toward it by at most 8° a tick. A target dead astern gets a level turn, not a loop.
- **Fuses:** contact detonates it, and so does coming within 1.5 blocks of its target's box. A contact within 4 blocks of launch is a dud: a puff, no blast, nothing dropped. The owner can't be struck for its first 10 ticks.
- **Blast:** the game's explosion with the rocket as its source. Vanilla credits a projectile's owner, so kills count and PvP applies.
- **Config:** `rocket.power` (default 3, a creeper's) and `rocket.breakBlocks` (default true) are a new server config, `rangedweaponsmod-server.toml` in the world. They are a world rule, so they belong to the server. The feed mode stays each player's (D-0024). NeoForge drops that file's stale `ammunition.feed` key on first load.

**Costs:**

| Item | Recipe | Yields |
|---|---|---|
| Launch tube | eight steel in a ring | 1 |
| Seeker | two gold ingots and a diamond over a scope, over a block of redstone | 1 |
| Launcher | the seeker over a stock, a lower receiver and the tube | 1 |
| Rocket | redstone over steel–TNT–steel over blaze powder | 1 |

In raw materials:
- **Launcher:** 13 iron, 2 gold, a diamond, a block of redstone, 2 glass panes, wood.
- **Rocket:** 3 iron, a TNT, a blaze powder.

The rockets form a new ammunition family, `rangedweaponsmod:ammo/rocket`. That follows the protocol's convention that a mod's family is in its own namespace; the plan had written `rangedweapons:ammo/rocket`. The family is added to `rangedweapons:ammo`.

**The client:**
- **HUD:** draws, with plain fills:
  - a ring while the sight is up;
  - amber brackets on the candidate, closing as the lock nears;
  - a red diamond on the locked target wherever it is, with an edge marker when it is off-screen or behind.
  
  Targets are placed through the camera's own axes and the world's field of view as last computed, not the render matrices, so a shader pack doesn't take the marks away.
- **Tones:** a growl rising with the acquisition, a steady tone while locked, and a drop-out when a lock is lost rather than fired.
- **Rocket:** drawn as the model `item/rocket_projectile`, with a smoke trail and the motor's loop. It is visible to 160 blocks; vanilla culls by size, which hid it at 19.

The network version goes from "3" to "4", for the synced seeker.

## Why

- **The launcher as a gun.** Every rule a player already knows (the trigger, the reload from what they carry, the counter, creative's unlimited ammunition) applies with nothing new.
- **The seeker on the server.** A lock decides which entity a rocket kills, so the client can't be the judge of it.
- **The lock and its targets as pure records.** Each of Rusty's rules is then a JUnit case.
- **Lead pursuit with a capped turn.** It meets a target moving at constant velocity as proportional navigation would, has no gain to tune, and its turn cap is one number that decides how dodgeable a rocket is.
- **The blast through the game's explosion.** The owner's credit, the PvP rule, Warehouse Manager's blast protection of claimed chests, and Dynamite's precedent for the switch all come with it.

## Rejected

- **Vanilla's item-use path for the held right click**, which bows and spyglasses use. It puts the player in use, so attack clicks are discarded and the trigger couldn't fire while locking. It also slows the player and dips the hand on every repeat.
- **Proportional navigation (N = 3)**, which the plan named. See above.
- **A new seek payload.** The aim message already says the sight is up, and the server already validates it.
- **A seeker judged on the client.** As above.
- **Locking the render matrices for the HUD.** A shader pack can replace the stage that would supply them.
- **Spread on rockets.** A rocket leaves the tube true, and the profile's spread is 0.

## Not decided here

- **The art and the sound are placeholders and ship in no release.** Vanilla textures stand in on cuboid models, and the sound events point at vanilla ones. Astra's brief is `devtools/art/ROCKET_LAUNCHER_BRIEF.md`.
- **The feel numbers are Rusty's to tune by hand:** 30-tick lock, 8° turn, speed 2.5, power 3.
- **Automobility's cars can be locked but take no damage at all.** That mod gives neither the car nor its hitbox a `hurt`. A rocket still goes off there and hurts the driver and anyone near it.

## Verified

- **JUnit:** `SeekerTest`, `GuidanceTest` (whole flights through the real motor and turn cap: a target dead astern, a sprinting target crossing, an aircraft crossing high at 1.2 blocks a tick), `MotorTest`, `TargetFactsTest`, and `BlueprintsTest`'s launcher cases. 173 in all.
- **GameTests (`LauncherGameTests`):**
  - a cow locks at exactly 30 ticks; a boat and a minecart lock; an armour stand, an item frame and bare blocks never do;
  - a lock outlives the look, the lowered sight and ten seconds, and ends with its target;
  - a locked rocket launched straight down the range turns onto a cow eight blocks off its line, moving, and kills it; the lock is spent;
  - an unlocked rocket keeps its heading exactly, and a cow off its line is untouched;
  - a locked boat is broken;
  - the blast breaks a pillar with the switch on, and leaves it with the switch off while still killing beside it;
  - a wall within the arming distance gets a dud;
  - creative fires with no rocket; an empty launcher loads one from a carried bag;
  - the off hand's item use is refused under the launcher and only under it.
  
  75 GameTests in all. Each new rule was run once against a mutation; the results are in the release record.
- **Recipe collisions:** `devtools/recipes/collisions.py` against the 115 jars of Rusty's pack finds none.

## Amended 2026-10-02: what the booth's biplane taught

Rusty: "I think part of the test booth should be a plane flying in the sky and a player locking
onto it and shooting it." The launcher booth (`-PboothLauncher`) got that scene: Immersive
Aircraft's biplane crossing the sky, the camera swinging with it through the use key's lock and the
attack key's launch, and checks that it is acquired, locked and brought down. It found three
things.

**1. Contact is judged on the client, and the server validates it.** As first built, the server
judged reticle contact: the player's aim, as it reached the server, against the target where the
server had it. A player aims at the target where their client draws it, which trails the server
by the connection and the smoothing. In the booth the client drew the plane 8 to 10 ticks behind
the server. At 0.8 blocks a tick that is 8 blocks, about 11° off a 2.5° cone, so the plane never
locked. A cow locked fine, being slow. On the box, with real ping, fast aircraft would have been
nearly impossible.

Now:
- The holder's client runs the reticle search on what it draws (`client/SeekReport`, the same
  `Seeking.inReticle`) and reports the target, or none, on each change (`SeekPayload`).
- The server keeps the report (`ModData.SEEN`, transient, unsynced). It counts the report only for
  a valid target, within 128 blocks, that the server's eye can see, within 30° of the server's own
  view of the look (`Seeking.SANITY_CONE`). The cone is generous enough for a fast target on a slow
  connection, and refuses a target the player isn't facing.
- Rusty's rule now holds as he put it: reticle contact as the player sees it.
- Test: `aReportTheServerCannotStandIsRefused` reports, as a lying client would, a cow behind the
  player, a cow behind a wall, and an armour stand. Each is refused.

**2. Close enough is a hit.** The proximity fuse used to detonate up to 1.5 blocks short of the
target. Immersive Aircraft's planes scale blasts down to about 5 % against their health, so the
booth's biplane flew on through a burst beside it, while a contact burst downed it. The rocket now
detonates on the target's nearest face when the fuse trips: the warhead closes that last stretch
within the tick. Contact and proximity strike equally hard.

**3. Immersive Aircraft runs only in the booth.** On the gametest server it sends its own payload
to every mock player that joins, and their embedded connections refuse it. That broke an unrelated
crafting test. So:
- The build copies the pack's exact jar (Modrinth `RkWu0N4D`, sha1 `f9bc465d…`, the one in Rusty's
  instance) into `run/booth/mods` for `-PboothLauncher` runs only (`copyBoothMods`), and removes it
  for every other booth.
- Headless, the tag's path is proven by `anEntityTheTagNamesIsLockedOnto`: the gametest pack's
  own copy of the tag names an end crystal, which is neither living nor a vanilla vehicle.

Two facts about the booth itself:
- Its render distance of 3 chunks dropped the plane at about 55 blocks. The server sends a player
  only the entities within their view. The launcher booth raises it to 8 for its run and puts it
  back after. Immersive Aircraft itself tracks its aircraft to 12 chunks, so play has no such limit.
- Scripting the plane's position while its own velocity stood made it fly twice as fast. The
  course zeroes the velocity.

77 GameTests and the launcher booth's 14 checks pass.
