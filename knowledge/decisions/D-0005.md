# D-0005 — The press is vanilla's use, so interactions win; the release and the clock stay ours

**Decision**: The client no longer cancels the use key for a gun. Vanilla's
right-click runs as it always does -- the block or entity under the crosshair
and in reach gets the click first -- and only when nothing takes it does
`GunItem.use` run, returning consume (no swing, no "using") and, on the
server, telling the gunnery the trigger is held. That call is
edge-triggered, since vanilla repeats a held use every four ticks. The
release is still this mod's message on the key coming up, and the fire clock
is still the server's tick.

**Why**: Rusty found that a gun in hand made doors and villagers dead to
right-click. D-0002 cancelled the click to escape vanilla's four-tick repeat
cap and the movement slowdown of an item in use; both are escaped just as
well by never calling `startUsingItem` and by keeping our own clock and
release, while the press itself can be vanilla's, which brings its reach and
interactivity rules for free. A client-side guess at "is this block
interactive" would have duplicated vanilla's decision badly.

**Alternatives rejected**: *Cancel unless the crosshair is on a container or
door* — a list that is wrong for every mod's blocks. *Interact on shift-click
only* — a habit nobody has.

**Follow-ups**: none.
